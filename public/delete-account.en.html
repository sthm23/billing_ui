Delete your STHM account

STHM app (Android, com.sthm23.billingmobile) · developer: Sanjar Tukhtamishev

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to sanjartukhtamishev@gmail.com with the subject “Account deletion”.
  2. Include the phone number linked to your account and the name of your store.
  3. We will confirm the request by email and delete the account within 30 days.

You can also ask the owner or administrator of your store to delete your account.

What is deleted

  • full name, phone number, login, password;
  • profile photo;
  • active sign-in sessions.

What is kept

Records of sales, payments and cash operations belong to the store and are needed for its accounting. They are kept in anonymized form (without your name or phone number) for no longer than 5 years after the store stops using the system, and are then deleted.

More details in the Privacy Policy.
