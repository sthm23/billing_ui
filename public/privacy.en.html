STHM Mobile App Privacy Policy

Effective date: 05.11.2026 · Last updated: 05.11.2026

This Privacy Policy (the “Policy”) explains what data the STHM mobile app (Android, package com.sthm23.billingmobile, the “App”) collects and processes, why it is used, how it is stored and protected, and what rights users have.

Developer and data controller: Sanjar Tukhtamishev (individual), Tashkent, Republic of Uzbekistan.
Privacy contact: sanjartukhtamishev@gmail.com.

1. Purpose of the App

STHM is a work tool for store staff and owners (a point-of-sale system): product and stock management, sales, payments, returns, cash register operations and tracking of customer debts.

The App is intended only for store employees who have been given an account by the store owner. The App is not intended for children and is not used to sell goods directly to end customers.

2. Data we process

2.1. Employee data (App users)

DataPurpose
Full nameShown in the profile; identifies who performed an operation (sale, payment, return)
Phone number, loginIdentification and sign-in
PasswordAuthentication. The password is sent over an encrypted connection and stored on the server only as a hash
Profile photo (optional)Shown in the profile
Role, store, warehousesAccess control
Activity history (sales, payments, cash operations)Accounting, inventory records and audit

2.2. Store customer data

Store employees may enter customer data into the system:

  • full name and phone number;
  • purchase history;
  • information about debts and their repayment.

This data is entered by an employee on behalf of the store. The store (the owner of the store account) is responsible for having a lawful basis to process it, including the customer’s consent where the law requires it.

2.3. Store business data

Products, prices, stock levels, orders, payments and cash operations. This data is not personal data, but it is protected to the same standard.

2.4. Technical data on the device

The App stores the following in its local storage on the device:

  • an authorization token (so that you do not have to enter your password on every launch);
  • the current user’s profile data;
  • interface settings (theme, language).

This data is deleted when you sign out or uninstall the App.

2.5. What we do not collect

  • location;
  • contacts, SMS, call logs;
  • photos or files from the gallery (except a profile photo if you upload one yourself);
  • audio (microphone access is not requested);
  • advertising identifiers;
  • bank card details (the App records only the fact and the amount of a payment, never the card number).

The App contains no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking.

3. App permissions

PermissionWhat it is used for
CameraOnly for scanning product barcodes. The image is processed on the device in real time and is neither stored nor sent to the server; only the recognized product code is sent
NotificationsLocal notifications while you work in the App (for example, the result of an operation). No push campaigns or marketing messages are sent
InternetExchanging data with the system’s server

You can revoke the camera and notification permissions at any time in the device settings; the rest of the App will keep working.

4. Purposes of processing

Data is processed solely to:

  1. provide the App’s functionality (inventory, sales, payments, debts);
  2. authenticate users and control access;
  3. ensure security and prevent unauthorized access;
  4. comply with accounting legislation (keeping the history of operations).

We do not sell data, do not share it with advertisers and do not use it for profiling.

5. Sharing with third parties

Data is not shared with third parties, except:

  • the hosting provider JSC “Uzbektelecom”, whose servers host the system (the servers are located in the Republic of Uzbekistan);
  • where expressly required by law (upon a lawful request from authorized government bodies).

One store’s data is not accessible to employees of other stores.

6. Storage and security

  • Data is stored on servers located in the Republic of Uzbekistan and is not transferred outside the country.
  • Data between the App and the server is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored only as hashes.
  • Access is role-based: an employee sees only the data of their own store and the warehouses assigned to them.
  • Sessions have a limited lifetime and can be terminated.

Retention periods. Data is kept no longer than necessary for the purposes of processing:

  • employee account — while it is active. After deletion, personal data (name, phone, login, photo) is erased within 30 days;
  • customer data (name, phone) — while the store uses the system or until the customer or the store requests deletion; then erased within 30 days;
  • records of sales, payments, debts and cash operations — while the store uses the system. After the store stops using it, the data is kept for another 5 years (the retention period for primary accounting documents under the laws of the Republic of Uzbekistan) so that the store can request it, and is then deleted. In records that remain after an account or customer is deleted, personal data is anonymized;
  • data on the device — until you sign out or uninstall the App.

If the service is discontinued, users will be notified at least 30 days in advance and will be able to request an export of their store’s data. After that, all data will be deleted.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • know what data about you is processed;
  • obtain a copy of it;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data deleted (subject to section 8);
  • withdraw your consent to processing.

To exercise these rights, email sanjartukhtamishev@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days.

Store customers whose data was entered into the system may contact either the store or us at the address above.

8. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account by:

  1. contacting the owner/administrator of your store; or
  2. emailing sanjartukhtamishev@gmail.com with the subject “Account deletion” and the phone number linked to your account.

Once the request is confirmed, the account and personal data (name, phone, photo, login) are deleted within 30 days. Records of completed financial operations may be kept in anonymized form for the period required by accounting legislation.

Step-by-step instructions: sthm23.uz/delete-account.en.html

9. Children

The App is not intended for persons under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Governing law

Personal data is processed in accordance with Law of the Republic of Uzbekistan No. ZRU-547 “On Personal Data” of 02.07.2019 and other legislation of the Republic of Uzbekistan. If the language versions of this Policy differ, the Russian version prevails.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy. The current version is always available at https://sthm23.uz/privacy.en.html; the date of the last update is shown at the top. We will notify you of material changes through the App.
